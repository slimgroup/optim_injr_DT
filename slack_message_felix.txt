**Key insights:**
1. **Alpha-epsilon correspondence**: CVaR's α parameter corresponds directly to POF's ε, allowing us to target the same tail probability level
2. **Gamma controls distribution width**: By adjusting γ, we can control the range of the optimized injection rate distribution:
   - **Small γ (e.g., 0.01, 0.0)**: CVaR produces narrower distributions than POF (more conservative/strict control)
   - **Large γ (e.g., 0.05, potentially larger)**: CVaR can produce wider distributions than POF (more permissive control)
3. **Flexibility advantage**: The side-by-side layout demonstrates that CVaR offers more flexibility than POF—we can tune γ to achieve either tighter or looser control compared to POF, depending on our risk tolerance

**Note on current results:**
The current figure shows gamma values up to 0.05. We observe that even at γ=0.05, some CVaR cases still show narrower distributions than POF, suggesting that to fully demonstrate CVaR's ability to achieve wider distributions than POF, we may need to explore larger gamma values (e.g., γ=0.1, 0.15).
