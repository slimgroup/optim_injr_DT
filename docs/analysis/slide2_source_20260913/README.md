## What was found

The strongest candidates are the **grey, textured coastal cutaway** reproduced in Pratama (2024), Figure 1, and Hardt (2025), Figure 5. They visually share the same underlying illustration, with different raster resolution/compression. All requested labels, five black well derricks, white compression vessels, the three-stack power station, blue sea, diagonal textured strata, orange upward and yellow downward well arrows are present. Hardt's embedded raster is 1357×802 (aspect 1.6920); Pratama's is 1071×635. The reported target aspect is 1.6931. This is a strong candidate family, not proof of identity with the unavailable target.

**Earliest verified occurrence of this candidate artwork located in this search:** Pratama's January **2024** thesis, printed p. 5 / PDF p. 20. This is an earliest *located reuse*, not a verified creation date or original author. Its caption points to Ali et al. (2022), but the inspected publisher-hosted Figure 3 from Ali is a different illustration. That chain remains unresolved.

## Figure-specific reuse terms

- **Pratama:** the thesis contains a general declaration about third-party permissions. Its permissions appendix (printed p. 95 / PDF p. 110) concerns the author's separate 2023 *Transport in Porous Media* article, not Figure 1 or Ali's review. It does not authorize reuse of this cutaway. [Inspected appendix](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/613908010.pdf#page=110).
- **Hardt:** the institutional record says “Kaikki oikeudet pidätetään” (all rights reserved). The third-party figure's upstream permission is not specified. Availability for download is not a figure reuse license. [LUT record](https://lutpub.lut.fi/handle/10024/169750).
- **Fan/Hu/Cheng:** the article declares CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, while Figure 1 explicitly credits Ali as a third-party source. This declaration does not resolve the upstream artwork's identity/rights or prove permission for a new derivative. [Publisher PDF, first and second pages](https://jf2.hep.com.cn/af80e18900174ffbb52d8768c5836aaf.pdf).
- **Ali:** the publisher-version text declares CC BY 4.0, corroborated by the [ECU institutional record](https://ro.ecu.edu.au/ecuworks2022-2026/50/). The KAUST cover sheet inconsistently lists BY-NC-ND in its item-license field. More fundamentally, the target/candidate has not been verified in the inspected Figure 3, so neither article license can yet be applied to it. The acknowledgments credit **Antonio García (KAUST) for the graphical abstract** (journal p. 21). This is a specific lead, not a credit for every illustration; the graphical abstract could not be retrieved and is not equated with the target. [Acknowledgments](https://repository.kaust.edu.sa/bitstreams/c49847ef-daeb-4cee-9035-2f78e181128d/download#page=22).
- **Bashir:** the displayed figure credits an earlier paper. No figure-specific permission granting rights to the grey target was verified.
- **ASEAN:** the report's copyright page allows credited reuse of ACE material but distinguishes third-party material. Its externally sourced Figure 5.1 therefore needs its own rights check. [Report copyright page](https://www.aseanenergy.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Report-CCS-Deployment-Framework-and-Roadmap.pdf#page=2).

## Search method and remaining work

No reverse-image upload/search capability was available, and the exact target file was absent. The search used quoted combinations of “Storage and Compression”, “Basalt Formation”, “Offshore Injection Well”, “Unmineable Coal Seams” and “Power Station”; keyword image searches; publisher/institutional PDFs; captions, reference lists, acknowledgments and copyright pages. Actual embedded images were extracted with `pdfimages` and inspected alongside rendered PDF pages. Text/image search results alone were not treated as an exact visual match.

Additional leads inspected or attempted include the 2008 *Elements* CCS issue, NETL's storage FAQ, a 2025 KTH thesis (download incomplete), and other indexed 2024–2025 theses. These do not establish an earlier verified original. Publisher/KAUST/ECU PDF retrieval and KAUST screenshots encountered 403/challenge/cache errors; these access limitations are distinct from a demonstrated absence of the candidate in every article version.

Next decisive inputs: (1) the actual slide-2 PNG or accessible original proposal PDF to confirm the candidate and crop; (2) an accessible publisher/institutional PDF or graphical abstract for Ali's paper to resolve its figure-version mismatch; (3) a figure-specific original credit/permission trail, potentially clarified by the cited authors or illustrator. Contacting anyone requires a separate instruction; none has been contacted.
