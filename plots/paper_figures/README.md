# Paper figure exports

For the selected figures, open [the current gallery](../latest/index.html) or
[the selected-file list](../latest/README.md). This directory also contains
original export bundles and reproduction inputs; its contents are not all
current manuscript selections.

The [asset registry](../../docs/reference/paper_assets.json) records selected
PNG paths, producers, and hashes. [Reading notes](../../docs/figures/README.md)
are centralized under `docs/`; their old paths here are compatibility links.
Manifests, validation reports, arrays, and checksums preserve provenance and may
be required by later renderers. Do not remove them solely because they are not
images. The [cleanup record](../../docs/reference/DELETION_REVIEW_2026-10-03.md)
lists approved removals and retained dependencies.
